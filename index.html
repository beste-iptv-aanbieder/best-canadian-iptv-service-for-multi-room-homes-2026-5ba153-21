Budget Resellers – What to Watch Out For

These services are often cheap but run on overcrowded servers. When many users stream at the same time – like on weekend football afternoons – you will see heavy buffering. In a multi‑room home, one freezing stream can ruin the experience for everyone. Picture quality is usually 720p or unstable HD. Activation can be slow, and payments may only be through cryptocurrency or bank transfer with no buyer protection.

Expat / International Server Lines

These services offer a wide range of channels from different countries. However, the servers are often far from Canada, leading to high latency. A common issue is audio desync – the sound falls behind the picture. For families with kids in one room and parents in another, this gets annoying quickly. Streams may drop from 1080p to lower resolution during peak times.

VOD‑Only Providers

Some companies focus on movies and TV shows on demand. Their live TV streams – including sports – are often unreliable. If your household wants to watch live events like news or games in multiple rooms, a VOD‑only service will not deliver. Their movie catalogues may be large, but the live streams use low bitrates and suffer from frequent buffering.
